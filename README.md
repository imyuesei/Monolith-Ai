# Notch Eyes

An Android app that puts a black "dynamic notch" with glowing, animated ring eyes on your home screen.

- **Full-screen eyes** in the app: they look around, blink, and smile. Touch to make them follow your finger, tap to make them smile.
- **Floating notch** over the home screen: a black pill at the top with little eyes. Tap it to expand (shows time and battery), long-press to make it smile. It also pops open when you unlock the phone or plug in or unplug the charger.
- Adjust notch size and distance from the top in the app.

## Download

[NotchEyes.apk](https://github.com/imyuesei/Monolith-Ai/releases/latest/download/NotchEyes.apk) is built by GitHub Actions on every push.

## Build locally

```bash
gradle :app:assembleDebug
```
